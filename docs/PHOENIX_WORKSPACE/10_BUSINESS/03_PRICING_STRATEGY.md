# Phoenix — Pricing Strategy

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Owner: Phoenix Project

Last Updated: July 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the pricing philosophy and commercial strategy for Phoenix.

Rather than focusing solely on subscription fees, the objective is to establish a pricing model aligned with the value created for customers.

This strategy supports:

- sustainable growth
- customer acquisition
- long-term profitability
- international scalability

---

# 2. Pricing Philosophy

Phoenix does not sell searches.

Phoenix sells:

- time saved
- broader market visibility
- reduced search effort
- faster product discovery
- better purchasing opportunities

Customers pay for efficiency, not for technology.

---

# 3. Pricing Objectives

The pricing model must:

- encourage user adoption
- demonstrate value quickly
- minimize purchase friction
- support recurring revenue
- remain simple to understand
- scale internationally

---

# 4. Pricing Principles

The pricing strategy follows these principles:

Value-based pricing

Simple plans

Transparent limits

No hidden fees

Predictable subscriptions

Easy upgrades

No vendor lock-in

# 15. Strategic Pricing Statement

Phoenix pricing reflects customer value rather than platform usage.

Customers subscribe because Phoenix consistently saves time, expands market visibility, and simplifies complex searches.

The platform succeeds when customer success exceeds subscription cost.
