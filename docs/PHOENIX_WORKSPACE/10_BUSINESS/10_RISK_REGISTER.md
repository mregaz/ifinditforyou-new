# 1. Purpose

This document identifies, classifies, and manages the principal risks associated with the Phoenix platform.

Its objective is to support proactive decision-making, improve resilience, and reduce operational uncertainty throughout the platform lifecycle.

Risk management is an ongoing activity integrated into product development, business strategy, and platform operations.

---

# 2. Risk Management Principles

Phoenix follows these principles:

Identify risks early.

Assess impact objectively.

Prioritize mitigation.

Continuously monitor.

Review regularly.

Learn from incidents.

Risk management is a continuous process rather than a one-time exercise.
