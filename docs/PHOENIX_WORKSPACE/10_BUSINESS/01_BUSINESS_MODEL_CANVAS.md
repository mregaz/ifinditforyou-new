- Long-term Product Strategy

The canvas follows the Business Model Canvas methodology while being adapted to the specific characteristics of Phoenix.

---

# 2. Vision

Phoenix aims to become the leading intelligent search platform capable of discovering products, vehicles, collectibles and rare items across multiple online marketplaces with a single search.

Instead of replacing existing marketplaces, Phoenix aggregates them into one intelligent discovery engine.

The long-term vision is to become the "Search Layer of the Classified Web."

# 4. Value Proposition

Phoenix provides:

One search.

Multiple marketplaces.

Intelligent ranking.

Time savings.

Better discovery.

Instead of manually checking numerous websites, users obtain a consolidated search result ranked by relevance.

Key benefits include:

- less time searching
- broader market coverage
- higher probability of finding rare products
- cleaner interface
- unified experience

# 15. Long-Term Strategic Position

Phoenix does not aim to compete with marketplaces.

Instead,

Phoenix becomes the discovery layer above them.

Marketplaces remain supply providers.

Phoenix becomes the intelligence layer.
