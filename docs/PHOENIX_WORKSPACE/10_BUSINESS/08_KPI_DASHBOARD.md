# Phoenix — KPI Dashboard

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Owner: Phoenix Project

Last Updated: July 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used to measure the health, growth, operational efficiency, and long-term success of Phoenix.

The dashboard provides a standardized framework for executive decision-making.

KPIs are organized into strategic categories and reviewed at predefined intervals.

---

# 2. KPI Philosophy

Phoenix measures outcomes rather than activity.

Success is determined by customer value, financial sustainability, operational excellence, and platform reliability.

Every KPI should support informed decision-making.
