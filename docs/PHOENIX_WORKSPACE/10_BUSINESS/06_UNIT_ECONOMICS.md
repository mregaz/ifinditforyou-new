# Phoenix — Unit Economics

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Owner: Phoenix Project

Last Updated: July 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the unit economics framework for Phoenix.

Its objective is to measure the financial sustainability of customer acquisition, retention, and long-term growth.

Rather than relying on fixed assumptions, Phoenix continuously evaluates key financial metrics to support data-driven decisions.

---

# 2. Financial Philosophy

Phoenix is designed as a scalable subscription business.

Growth must improve financial performance rather than increase operational inefficiency.

Every additional customer should contribute positively to long-term profitability.

---

# 3. Core Metrics

The following indicators represent the primary unit economics metrics.

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

Lifetime Value (LTV)

Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)

Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)

Gross Margin

Contribution Margin

Customer Churn

Retention Rate

Payback Period

---

# 4. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

CAC measures the average investment required to acquire one paying customer.

Includes:

Marketing

Advertising

Sales

Referral incentives

Promotional campaigns

Content production

Phoenix aims to reduce CAC through:

SEO

Organic growth

Referrals

Brand recognition

Product-led growth

---

# 5. Lifetime Value (LTV)

LTV measures the total expected revenue generated by a customer during their relationship with Phoenix.

LTV increases through:

Long retention

High customer satisfaction

Feature expansion

Premium subscriptions

Low churn

Future upgrades

Increasing LTV is more sustainable than constantly acquiring new customers.

---

# 6. Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)

ARPU measures average recurring revenue generated by each paying customer.

Growth drivers include:

Pro subscriptions

Business plans

Enterprise contracts

API usage

Premium services

The objective is to increase ARPU by delivering additional value rather than increasing prices alone.

---

# 7. Gross Margin

Gross Margin measures the profitability of delivering the service.

Direct costs include:

Infrastructure

Hosting

Database

Storage

Bandwidth

Third-party services

Payment processing

Phoenix targets high gross margins through:

Cloud efficiency

Automation

Reusable architecture

Operational simplicity

---

# 8. Customer Retention

Retention represents one of Phoenix's most important business metrics.

Retention improves through:

Reliable search quality

Provider expansion

Excellent user experience

Saved searches

Alerts

Continuous product improvements

Customer success

High retention compounds business value over time.

---

# 9. Customer Churn

Churn measures customer loss.

Potential causes include:

Poor search quality

Limited provider coverage

Slow platform evolution

Pricing dissatisfaction

Technical instability

Weak onboarding

Phoenix continuously monitors churn and addresses root causes.

---

# 10. Payback Period

Payback Period measures how long it takes to recover CAC.

Shorter payback enables:

Faster growth

Lower financing needs

Greater resilience

Higher profitability

The objective is continuous reduction through operational improvements.

---

# 11. Contribution Margin

Contribution Margin measures the financial contribution of each customer after variable costs.

Positive contribution margin enables:

Platform expansion

Product investment

Marketing growth

Future innovation

Every additional customer should strengthen platform economics.

---

# 12. Scaling Economics

Phoenix benefits from economies of scale.

As customer volume grows:

Infrastructure efficiency improves.

Brand awareness increases.

Referral acquisition grows.

Documentation scales.

Provider integrations benefit all users.

Average operational cost per customer decreases.

---

# 13. Financial Targets

Long-term objectives include:

Positive contribution margin

Strong gross margin

Growing ARPU

Low CAC

High LTV

Low churn

Predictable recurring revenue

Sustainable profitability

These targets guide strategic decision-making.

# 15. Decision Framework

Strategic decisions should prioritize improvements in:

Customer retention

LTV

Gross margin

Contribution margin

Operational efficiency

Revenue quality

Growth without improving these indicators is not considered sustainable.

---

# 16. Unit Economics Principles

Phoenix evaluates success using the following principles:

Recurring revenue over one-time revenue.

Retention over acquisition.

Customer value over price increases.

Operational efficiency over uncontrolled expansion.

Sustainable growth over rapid growth.

Financial discipline over optimistic assumptions.
