# 4. Target Audience

Phoenix is designed for people who need to find specific products quickly, reliably and across multiple sources.

Typical users include:

- Private buyers
- Collectors
- Vehicle enthusiasts
- Watch enthusiasts
- Technology buyers
- Professionals searching for equipment
- People looking for rare or discontinued products

The common characteristic is simple:

They value quality results more than spending hours searching manually.

---

# 5. User Personas

## Persona 1 — Everyday Buyer

Needs:

- Save time
- Compare multiple sources
- Receive reliable results

Goals:

- Find the right product quickly
- Avoid repetitive searches

---

## Persona 2 — Collector

Needs:

- Rare items
- Hard-to-find products
- Continuous monitoring

Goals:

- Never miss an opportunity
- Discover listings before others

---

## Persona 3 — Professional

Needs:

- Efficient research
- Reliable information
- Productivity

Goals:

- Reduce manual work
- Make informed purchasing decisions

---

## Persona 4 — Power User

Needs:

- Multiple searches
- Search history
- Saved alerts
- Advanced filters

Goals:

- Maximize efficiency
- Automate repetitive tasks

# 6. Positioning

Phoenix is positioned as an intelligent search platform that helps users discover products across multiple marketplaces through a single experience.

Phoenix is not simply another marketplace.

Phoenix is not another classified website.

Phoenix connects users with opportunities wherever they exist.

The value lies in aggregation, intelligence and simplicity.

---

# 7. Competitive Position

Phoenix does not compete by having the largest inventory.

It competes by offering:

- Better search experience
- Better organization
- Better discovery
- Better transparency
- Better efficiency

Competitors own listings.

Phoenix helps users navigate them.

---

# 8. Value Proposition

Phoenix helps users:

- Save time
- Search multiple sources
- Reduce manual effort
- Discover opportunities faster
- Make better purchasing decisions

The promise is simple:

**Less searching. Better finding.**

---

# 9. Decision Framework

Before introducing a new feature ask:

- □ Does it benefit our target users?
- □ Does it simplify their experience?
- □ Does it reinforce our positioning?
- □ Does it strengthen our competitive advantage?
- □ Does it create measurable value?

If the answer is "No", the feature should be reconsidered.
