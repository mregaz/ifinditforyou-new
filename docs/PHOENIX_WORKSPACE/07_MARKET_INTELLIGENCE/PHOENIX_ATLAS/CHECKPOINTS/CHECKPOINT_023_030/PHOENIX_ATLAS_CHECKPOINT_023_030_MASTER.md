## Strategic synthesis

The 23–30 block materially strengthens five Phoenix architecture themes:

1. **Access is a first-class capability.** Public HTML does not imply permission to automate. Provider access must distinguish API, authorized feed, partnership, HTML authorization and NO-GO.
2. **Freshness and ranking require provenance.** Renewals, paid resurfacing and source position cannot be treated as chronological freshness or relevance.
3. **Provider priority is granular.** Value belongs to Provider × Country × Vertical, using fresh Phoenix-eligible inventory rather than raw totals.
4. **Provider families are broader than brands.** Locale surfaces can migrate, hostnames can encode geography, and families may span different brands/domains.
5. **Knowledge providers can be more valuable than inventory providers.** Idealista demonstrates that historical data, demand, comparables and valuations can move Phoenix from search toward Decision Intelligence.
