## Channel scorecard

Score weekly:

```text
qualified_users
WQSS
founder_hours
WQSS_per_5_founder_hours
return_or_monitoring_intent
referrals
community_complaints
cash_cost
```

### Continue

- ≥1 WQSS per 5 founder hours;
- no rule violation;
- repeatable access to similar users.

### Stop

- 10 qualified contacts with zero completed search;
- moderator warning;
- audience mismatch;
- content visits without search starts;
- source access requires unauthorized automation.

## Evidence notes

- Swiss community discussions repeatedly list MotoScout24, Ricardo, Tutti, Anibis and Facebook as separate places users check ([used motorbike marketplaces](https://www.reddit.com/r/askswitzerland/comments/196k63g), **C**).
- Product Hunt says launch is free, self-hunting is normal and direct upvote solicitation is prohibited ([official guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch), **A**).
- Product Hunt advises becoming a community participant before launch rather than arriving only to promote ([preparation guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/before-launch), **A**).
- Show HN should expose something people can try, not a landing page or announcement alone ([official guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html), **A**).
