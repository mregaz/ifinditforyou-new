Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Owner: Phoenix Project

Last Updated: July 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines how knowledge evolves throughout the lifecycle of the Phoenix platform.

Its objectives are:

- Preserve organizational knowledge.
- Support continuous improvement.
- Maintain documentation quality.
- Capture lessons learned.
- Reduce knowledge loss.
- Ensure long-term sustainability.

Knowledge is considered a strategic asset of the Phoenix project.

---

# 2. Evolution Principles

Phoenix knowledge evolves according to the following principles:

Continuous learning

Incremental improvement

Traceability

Documentation First

Single Source of Truth

Knowledge preservation

Every significant improvement should increase the quality of the Knowledge Base.

---

# 15. Strategic Conclusion

Phoenix recognizes knowledge as one of its most valuable long-term assets.

By governing the continuous evolution of documentation, architecture, business strategy, operations, and decision history, the project ensures that its collective knowledge remains accurate, accessible, and sustainable throughout the entire lifecycle of the platform.

The true value of Phoenix is not only the software it delivers, but also the knowledge it preserves and continuously improves.
