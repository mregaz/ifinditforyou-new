# 1. Purpose

This document provides a high-level business view of the Phoenix platform.

Its objectives are:

- Present the business ecosystem.
- Describe value creation.
- Explain revenue generation.
- Identify strategic stakeholders.
- Support business planning.
- Connect business strategy with platform architecture.

The Business Map complements the Architecture Map by describing how Phoenix creates, delivers, and captures value.

---

# 2. Business Philosophy

Phoenix is designed to become a universal search platform for online marketplaces.

Its mission is to simplify the discovery of products across multiple independent sources through a unified search experience.

Business success depends on delivering measurable value to users while maintaining a scalable and sustainable business model.

# 4. Core Stakeholders

Phoenix serves multiple stakeholder groups.

---

## End Users

Individuals searching for products across multiple marketplaces.

Objectives:

Save time

Find better offers

Access multiple sources

Receive high-quality search results

End users are the primary beneficiaries of the platform.

---

## Subscribers

Customers paying for premium features.

Benefits include:

Unlimited searches

Advanced filtering

Priority features

Future premium services

Subscribers generate recurring revenue.

---

## Marketplace Providers

External platforms integrated through the Provider SDK.

Objectives:

Increase visibility

Improve content accessibility

Expand user reach

Phoenix acts as an intelligent aggregation layer rather than replacing marketplace providers.

---

## Business Partners

Potential strategic partners.

Examples:

Affiliate partners

Commercial partners

Technology providers

Marketing partners

Business partnerships support ecosystem growth.
